# Enneagram 3w4

## Overview

PersonalityData.org describes 3w4 as a Type Three with a Four wing. Type Three is presented as achievement-oriented, energetic and competitive, with attention directed toward completing goals and receiving recognition. The Four wing adds a stronger emphasis on individual expression, creativity, openness and personal identity.

The page calls this combination the **Professional**. It describes 3w4 as retaining the drive and industriousness associated with Type Three while placing additional importance on expressing individuality and being recognized for personal achievements. The Four wing is also described as potentially making the person more emotionally open, vulnerable or introverted than the general Type Three description.

## Core Motivation, Fears and Desires

The source identifies recognition and success as central themes for this type.

### Core Fear

The page describes the central fear as failing in one's efforts and not doing what is necessary to succeed. It also lists concerns such as:

- Developing a poor reputation.
- Repeatedly failing to reach personal goals.
- Becoming disconnected or withdrawn from other people.

### Core Desire

The page describes the central desire as being recognized as successful and feeling valued for those achievements. Other desires listed include:

- Reaching the goals that have been set.
- Working hard for other people.
- Improving and understanding oneself.

## Characteristics

The page lists the following characteristics for 3w4:

- Seeking success and accomplishment.
- Paying attention to social image and how one is perceived.
- Worrying about being unworthy of affection.
- Being diligent and industrious.
- Being more introverted and open in self-expression than some other Type Threes.

The source also suggests that under stress or in an unhealthy state, a 3w4 may seek praise too strongly, potentially at the expense of integrity or harmony with others.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

| Strengths described by the source | Weaknesses described by the source |
|---|---|
| Diligence in pursuing goals | Becoming overly focused on professional success |
| Identifying opportunities for personal growth | Becoming excessively competitive |
| Hard work and industriousness | Seeking praise too aggressively |
| Connecting with and charming other people | Becoming too withdrawn or focused on self-image |

The page adds that the Four wing may moderate the outward bravado often associated with Type Three by introducing a more inward and self-aware perspective. It also notes a possible downside: feeling stressed in situations where the person is not the centre of attention.
